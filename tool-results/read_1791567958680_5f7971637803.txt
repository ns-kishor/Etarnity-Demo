    15→# 1. Project Overview
    16→
    17→## 1.1 Project Vision
    18→
    19→Build the official corporate website of **ETARNITY**, designed to serve as the central digital headquarters of the company and its broader business ecosystem.
    20→
    21→This website must communicate what ETARNITY is, what it does, what it builds, how it operates, who leads it, which businesses and products it owns or operates, and how it plans to grow.
    22→
    23→ETARNITY must not be presented merely as a web development agency, marketing agency, or service marketplace.
    24→
    25→It must be positioned as a technology company with the potential to build and operate multiple digital businesses, products, platforms, services, and technology initiatives.
    26→
    27→The website should be designed around three core principles:
    28→
    29→1. **Corporate Identity:** Establish a clear and credible understanding of ETARNITY.
    30→2. **Technology & Innovation:** Demonstrate the company's technical capabilities through its design, products, projects, and engineering philosophy.
    31→3. **Trust & Transparency:** Present accurate company information, real people, genuine work, and clear business relationships.
    32→
    33→The website must feel like a professional corporate platform built for an international audience—not a generic AI-generated landing page.
    34→
    35→## 1.2 Core Objective
    36→
    37→The website must help visitors understand the company without requiring extensive technical knowledge.
    38→
    39→Within the first few seconds, visitors should understand:
    40→
    41→* What ETARNITY is.
    42→* What the company does.
    43→* Which technology areas it operates in.
    44→* What products, services, businesses, and projects are associated with it.
    45→* Who founded and leads the company.
    46→* How to explore its businesses or contact the appropriate team.
    47→
    48→The website is primarily an **informational, corporate, and business-development platform**. It is not intended to be a direct e-commerce store.
    49→
    50→Individual products or businesses may link to their own websites, platforms, dashboards, or purchasing experiences.
    51→
    52→## 1.3 Definition of Success
    53→
    54→A successful website should:
    55→
    56→* Communicate ETARNITY's identity clearly.
    57→* Look credible beside established international technology companies.
    58→* Demonstrate real technical and design capability.
    59→* Make company information easy to discover.
    60→* Organize multiple business areas without overwhelming the homepage.
    61→* Support future businesses, products, employees, and organizational growth.
    62→* Be fully manageable through a secure admin panel.
    63→* Perform reliably across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

   116→# 3. Visual Design System
   117→
   118→## 3.1 Overall Design Direction
   119→
   120→Create a distinctive visual identity combining:
   121→
   122→* Premium corporate editorial design
   123→* Modern technology-company aesthetics
   124→* Refined typography
   125→* Purposeful motion
   126→* Minimal navigation
   127→* Sophisticated spatial composition

   129→* High-quality project imagery
   130→* Clear information hierarchy
   131→
   132→The website should not look like a conventional corporate template, a generic SaaS landing page, or a gaming interface.
   133→
   134→Every visual element must have a purpose.
   135→
   136→## 3.2 Color Palette
   137→
   138→Use a refined, technology-oriented palette.
   139→
   140→### Primary Colors
   141→
   142→* **Deep Graphite:** `#15171B`
   143→* **Warm White:** `#F8F8F6`
   144→* **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF`
   145→* **Cool Gray:** `#E8E9EC`
   146→* **Secondary Text:** `#646B76`
   147→
   148→### Accent Colors
   149→
   150→Use a restrained combination of:
   151→
   152→* **Electric Blue:** `#526BFF`
   153→* **Soft Periwinkle:** `#AAB7FF`
   154→
   155→The accent color may be refined during implementation to match the final visual direction.
   156→
   157→Use light surfaces for readability, dark sections for contrast, and accent colors sparingly.
   158→
   159→Do not force every section into a dark theme.
   160→
   161→The overall experience should feel balanced, clean, premium, and unmistakably technical.
   162→
   163→## 3.3 Typography
   164→
   165→Use professional fonts suitable for an international technology company.
   166→
   167→Recommended combinations:
   168→
   169→* **Primary:** Geist or Satoshi
   170→* **Alternative:** Inter or Space Grotesk
   171→* **Technical Metadata:** Geist Mono or IBM Plex Mono
   172→
   173→Typography rules:
   174→
   175→* Strong, confident hero typography.
   176→* Clear distinction between headings and body text.
   177→* Consistent spacing and line height.
   178→* Technical labels used sparingly.
   179→* Highly readable body copy.
   180→* No excessive font styles.
   181→* No decorative fonts that undermine technical credibility.
   182→
   183→## 3.4 Layout System
   184→
   185→Use a consistent responsive grid.
   186→
   187→* Maximum content width: approximately 1440px.
   188→* Generous but controlled whitespace.
   189→* Consistent horizontal margins.
   190→* Clear section separation.
   191→* Responsive typography.
   192→* Purposeful asymmetrical layouts.
   193→* Cards only where they improve understanding.
   194→* Minimal use of borders and shadows.
   195→
   196→The design must feel intentionally art-directed, not automatically assembled from repeated components.
